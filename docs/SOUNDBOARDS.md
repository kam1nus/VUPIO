# Boards for games and moments

**All sounds** is your complete library. Custom boards reference those same sounds; they do not duplicate audio files.

## Make a board

1. Open **Soundboard → New board**.
2. Name it, for example Minecraft, Ranked or Chill.
3. Search and check sounds from your library. You can create an empty board, too.
4. Choose **Create board**. Its tab appears above the cards.

Use **Manage board** to rename it and check/uncheck sounds. Search filters the picker, and **Select visible** adds the matching sounds to your selection. Unchecking removes a sound from this board only. Deleting a board keeps the sound files in All sounds and on other boards. Deleting an audio file in Sound details removes that sound from every board.

## Playback and organization

Click a card to trigger it. Preview, Edit, STOP, PANIC, categories and search work as before. Each board saves its own card order. Drag a handle to swap cards, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Stars remain library-wide favorites; favorites stay above other sounds on every board. Reorder within the same favorite group.

A board switch does not interrupt a currently playing sound. The player at the bottom still shows it, and STOP remains available.

## Adding new sounds

Select a board before uploading audio, using Discover Add/Quick Add, adding a Trend Radar sound or saving a Studio copy. The sound is saved in All sounds and attached to the selected board. Discover, Radar and Studio show the active import destination. A task uses the board selected when that add/save action starts, even if you switch boards while it finishes. If that board has meanwhile been deleted, the audio stays in All sounds and the app explains the attachment failure.

For an existing sound, choose **Boards** on its card:

- **Add to board** keeps the sound on its current boards and adds it to the destination. Already-added sounds are marked; you can add to several boards without closing the picker.
- **Move to board** removes the sound from the board you opened and adds it to the destination. All sounds and other board memberships stay intact. Moving is available inside a custom board, since All sounds is the full library.
- Search destination names, or enter a new board name and choose **Create & add / Create & move**. Creating and moving happen together, so a failed operation cannot leave a sound between boards.
- **Sound details…** opens title, tags, source/license, AI metadata and Delete sound. **Preview** and **Edit** remain directly on the card.

Use Manage board when selecting many sounds at once. Board changes do not restart audio, change favorites or duplicate files, and sync to the phone remote.

## Phone remote

Pair the remote as usual. Use the **Soundboard** dropdown above the tiles. Tap a tile to play it on the computer. The phone and desktop remember their own selections, so selecting a board on the phone does not switch the desktop. Edits to board names and contents sync to connected remotes automatically. Phone remotes cannot create, change, reorder or delete boards.

Boards and memberships are saved locally in the existing library database and survive app updates. Existing libraries begin in All sounds with their original files, favorites and global order intact.
