# VUPIO Pro ideas

These are ideas to test with users.

- **VUPIO Rooms:** A shared Soundboard for a group. A host invites friends by link, approves suggested sounds and controls who can play them. Guests can join from a browser. Start with one room and a host-owned library.
- **Sound Drops:** Viewers submit sounds through a link. A streamer gets a private queue for preview, approval and playback. Approved sounds can be saved to the library.
- **OBS Overlay:** Each sound can trigger a caption, image or short animation in an OBS browser source. The overlay ends with playback.
- **Voice Replay:** After explicit opt-in, keep a short rolling buffer of the user's own microphone in memory. A hotkey saves a recent phrase as a Studio draft. Display a clear listening indicator.

Earlier candidates include Trend Radar Pro, AI Copilot and Smart Clips.
